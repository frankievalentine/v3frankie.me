<a href="https://trybackroom.vercel.app" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Backroom</a> makes a Shopify store's public product catalog easier to explore. Paste a storefront URL to browse products alongside their variants, pricing, images, and metadata without creating an account or configuring an API key.

## What it does

- Loads the store's paginated public catalog rather than stopping at the first page.
- Filters products by vendor, product type, tags, and price.
- Keeps saved stores in the browser so they are easy to revisit.

## Why it matters

A storefront's navigation does not always make its catalog easy to explore. Backroom brings the published product data into one searchable view, making it easier to find products and compare variants across a store.
