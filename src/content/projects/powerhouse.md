powerhouse is about reducing the friction between an idea and a working AI-native development environment. Instead of hand-assembling every machine, toolchain, and workflow from scratch, the system starts from a curated registry of tools, profiles, and domains.

The emphasis is on composability. A good environment is rarely just a list of packages. It is a set of defaults, conventions, and context that fit the kind of work being done.

## Current focus

- Curate a registry that is actually useful, not just large.
- Make profiles and domains composable enough to adapt to real workflows.
- Favor repeatability so environment setup feels reliable instead of fragile.

## Why it matters

AI-assisted development changes what a workstation needs to do. powerhouse treats the environment itself as a product surface instead of an afterthought.
