spritedeck is a cloud-native canvas for teams working with Fly.io's sprites. The product is aimed at organizations that want a clearer way to shape, organize, and operate AI agents without reducing the experience to a pile of disconnected prompts and infrastructure settings.

The canvas model matters because agent work is easier to understand when structure is visible. Relationships, responsibilities, and execution context should be easier to inspect than they are in a terminal log or a set of ad hoc scripts.

## Current focus

- Make the canvas useful for real organizational workflows.
- Keep the cloud-native foundation aligned with how Fly.io sprites already operate.
- Design the interface around clarity and orchestration, not novelty.

## Why it matters

Agent tooling often feels either too abstract or too low-level. spritedeck is an attempt to give teams a surface that is operationally grounded and easier to collaborate around.
