ledgerkit is a business records tool for people who run small internet businesses and want a system they can actually understand. It is aimed at solo developers, consultants, indie hackers, and small SaaS operators who need clear records without handing over control of their data to a black-box platform.

## Current focus

- Keep the records model simple enough to understand at a glance.
- Preserve data ownership instead of pushing users into a closed system.
- Build a tool that feels useful for small operators instead of enterprise teams.

## Why it matters

Small businesses and independent operators still need clean records, but most tools in the space are either too bloated, too opaque, or too locked down. ledgerkit is an attempt to build something calmer, clearer, and easier to trust.
