openprexo is a local-first product for AI image and video generation, with a browser app, CLI, and desktop app. The browser and desktop apps provide a conversational workspace for creating media, while the CLI supports automation, agents, and repeatable generation workflows.

The interfaces share a local service that keeps prompts, jobs, conversations, and generated artifacts on your machine. You can submit image or video jobs, follow their progress, revisit conversations, and view or download completed media from one workflow.

Local model backends and bring-your-own-key cloud adapters connect to the same workflow, keeping your choice of tools alongside your prompts and outputs.

openprexo Cloud will let you generate images and videos with hosted models through one account, without running models on your own hardware or managing separate provider API keys. You'll choose Cloud models in the browser or desktop app, use generation credits across the model catalog, and manage your subscription, usage, and model preferences from the Cloud dashboard.

## Current focus

- Keep the browser app, CLI, and desktop app aligned around the same jobs and media.
- Refine the conversation, generation, and artifact workflow for both images and videos.
- Prepare cross-platform releases and continue validating local and cloud provider integrations.

## Why it matters

Creative work moves between hands-on experimentation and automated production. openprexo brings both into one system, so prompts, generation jobs, and outputs stay connected across the browser, terminal, and desktop.
